Switches, Relays & Power Isolation Modules 🔌

Switches and relays form the critical interface between low-power decision logic (microcontrollers operating at 3.3V or 5V DC) and high-power physical loads (such as industrial motors, solenoid valves, and 230V AC household appliances). Physical switches provide manual digital state inputs, while relays and solid-state modules allow low-voltage digital signals to safely switch high-current electric circuits without damaging sensitive logic chips.

Understanding the distinction between mechanical switching devices and semiconductor power controllers is essential for reliable circuit design. Electromechanical relays utilize internal electromagnetic coils to physically pull contacts together, offering complete physical isolation but experiencing contact arcing and mechanical wear over time. In contrast, Solid-State Relays (SSR) and MOSFET modules leverage semiconductor physics (such as Optocouplers and TRIACs) to achieve completely silent, high-frequency switching with zero moving parts.

Tactile Push Button Switches

Momentary Single Pole Single Throw (SPST) switches. Pressing the tactile cap bridges internal metallic domes to send instant digital HIGH/LOW state triggers to microcontroller input pins.

5V Optocoupler Relay Module

Electromechanical relay module with integrated PC817 optocoupler IC. Provides physical optical light-barrier isolation to shield microcontroller I/O pins from high-voltage AC back-EMF spikes.

⚡ Load: 250V AC / 10A | Opto-isolated | Active LOW/HIGH Trigger

Solid State Relay (SSR-40DA)

Semiconductor switching device utilizing internal zero-cross firing logic and TRIAC output. Operates without mechanical contacts, eliminating physical wear, arcing, and acoustic click noise.

🔥 Output: 24-380V AC / 40A | Control Input: 3-32V DC | Zero Noise
Official Datasheet 📄

IRF520 Power MOSFET Module

N-Channel power MOSFET capable of high-frequency PWM switching for DC motors, high-intensity LED strings, and heating elements with extremely low internal resistance.

⚡ Switching: 0-24V DC / 5A | PWM Compatible | Low RDS(on) Resistance

DIP Switch Arrays
